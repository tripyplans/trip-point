Jaipur Travel Guide

Useful information for planning your visit.

  • Best time to visit: October to March
  • Famous attractions: Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace
  • Local food: Dal Baati Churma, Ghewar and Pyaaz Kachori
  • Popular activities: Heritage tours, shopping and photography
  • Travel tip: Carry water and comfortable walking shoes.
