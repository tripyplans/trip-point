Kerala Travel Guide

Some useful details to plan your trip.

  • Best time to visit: October to March for many popular destinations
  • Famous places: Munnar, Alleppey, Kochi and Wayanad
  • Local food: Appam, puttu, Kerala sadya and banana chips
  • Popular activities: Houseboat trips, nature walks and sightseeing
  • Travel tip: Check local weather before planning outdoor activities.
